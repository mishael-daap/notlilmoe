# Roadmap

## 1. Landing page
- **Status**: in development
- **Goal**: A mobile-first single page that looks like the artist — hero, image slot, footer — with all copy and links coming from one config file.

## 2. Fan funnel
- **Status**: in development
- **Goal**: A name + email form on `/` that stores the fan and navigates to `/listen`, where Spotify and Apple Music are the two main items on screen, logos and all; tapping one records the choice and opens the artist profile.

## 3. Privacy policy
- **Status**: pending
- **Goal**: A short plain-language page stating what is collected (name, email, chosen platform), why, and how to be removed, linked from the form.

## 4. Deploy and go live
- **Status**: pending
- **Goal**: Ship to Vercel with Supabase in production, env vars set, and a real submission verified end to end on a phone.
